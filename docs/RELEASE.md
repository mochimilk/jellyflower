jellyflower 0.24.0

The taskbar has a new style, **fluted**, and it's now the default. Whatever is behind the taskbar shows through soft vertical reeds, the same glass as the menus, under your light or dark tint. With floating on, the dock's pill is fluted and the rest of the bar stays see-through. jellyflower draws the glass in its own window just behind the taskbar, so a window that opens or moves behind the bar shows through within a moment. **taskbar › acrylic** still offers system, pure and off, and your choice is now remembered after a restart. With energy saver on, transparency effects off or high contrast, the taskbar falls back to Windows' own look, as before.

The system tray has its own menu: **taskbar › system tray** holds where it sits, what it shows, the clock's date and, on narrow screens, the status bar. Set position to top for a bar along the top of every screen, like a Mac's menu bar: the flower at the left opens the jellyflower menu, and Quick Settings and the clock sit at the right. Windows still opens Quick Settings and notifications from the taskbar's corner, and app icons stay in the taskbar. The top bar is off by default.

Known issues: the fluted taskbar has been checked on one portrait touch tablet in dark mode with floating on; the full-width bar, light mode, auto hide, full-screen apps, more than one display and other display scales aren't checked yet. Something animating inside a window behind an auto-hidden bar updates in the glass only when windows open, close or move. The top bar has been checked piece by piece but not yet end to end on a desktop. As in 0.22.0, on a display with compact margins, selecting VS Code's maximize button has sometimes maximized the window and then closed it, and the status bar can't yet be reached with the keyboard or a screen reader.

The Explorer helper is now v21, and the Start/Search helper stays v21. A normally stopped 0.22.0 or 0.23.0 helper restores the taskbar before v21 attaches, so no sign-out is needed. Updates do not restart Explorer.
