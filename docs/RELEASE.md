jellyflower 0.22.0

The taskbar menu has a new **floating** row. Turn it on and the taskbar becomes a floating dock: Start and your apps sit in one rounded pill just above the bottom edge of the screen, with a thin outline, in the acrylic mode you chose. The taskbar grows from 48 to 64 pixels (at 100% scale), so the icons have room above and below, and each app's hover and open highlight becomes a rounded square, like an app icon. The rest of the taskbar turns see-through, and clicks and taps there reach the desktop, or with auto hide on, the window behind. The notification area stays at the right without a background. Turn floating off, or exit jellyflower, and Windows' taskbar comes back as it was.

To make the taskbar taller, jellyflower needs to know where Windows sizes it. The first time you turn floating on, and again after a Windows update changes the taskbar, it tells you in a notice, downloads Microsoft's public debugging symbols for the taskbar (about 7 MB, from Microsoft's symbol server), keeps two numbers and deletes the file. Nothing is downloaded while floating is off. If the download doesn't work, the dock still floats at Windows' usual height.

On screens too narrow for Start's menu, such as a tablet in portrait, jellyflower now keeps a margin around maximized and snapped windows: 8 pixels at the sides and above the taskbar, and 40 at the top, clear of rounded screen corners. Apps aren't changed; Windows just gives them a slightly smaller area. The top margin shows the time; select it and jellyflower's menu drops down from it, so the flower leaves the tray there. Turn the time off in **taskbar > status bar** and the margins stay. Rotating to a wider orientation removes them.

Known issues: on a display with these margins, selecting VS Code's maximize button has sometimes maximized the window and then closed it. The status bar can't yet be reached with the keyboard or a screen reader. The floating dock has been checked on one portrait touch tablet; multiple displays, other display scales, a left-aligned taskbar and auto hide are not checked yet.

The Explorer helper is now v20, and the Start/Search helper stays v21. A normally stopped 0.20.0 or 0.21.0 helper restores the taskbar before v20 attaches, so no sign-out is needed. Updates do not restart Explorer.
