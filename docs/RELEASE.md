jellyflower 0.23.0

jellyflower's menus are now fluted glass. Whatever is behind a menu shows through soft vertical reeds, each one bending the view like a ribbed glass panel, with a faint color fringe and a little light along one side, under the same light or dark tint as before. Text and icons stay crisp on top, and the submenus match. With energy saver on, menus use Windows' acrylic as before, and turning off transparency effects or turning on high contrast still makes them solid.

The glass is drawn by jellyflower itself, inside its own menus; the taskbar, Start and Search are unchanged. It adapts the fluted glass of the willow-ui design system.

Known issues: the glass has been checked on one portrait touch tablet in dark mode. Light mode, energy saver, other display scales and multiple displays are not checked yet. The issues listed for 0.22.0 remain: on a display with compact margins, selecting VS Code's maximize button has sometimes maximized the window and then closed it, and the status bar can't yet be reached with the keyboard or a screen reader.

The Explorer helper stays v20 and the Start/Search helper v21, so updating from 0.22.0 needs no sign-out. Updates do not restart Explorer.
